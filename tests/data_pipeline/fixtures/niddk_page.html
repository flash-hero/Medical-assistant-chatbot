How do health care professionals diagnose aplastic anemia?

Your health care professional will use your medical and family history, physical exam, and test results to diagnose your condition.

Blood tests

A complete blood count is usually the first blood test used. It measures

  • the amount of hemoglobin
  • the number of red blood cells and platelets
