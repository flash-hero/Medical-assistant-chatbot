Why get vaccinated?

HPV vaccine can prevent infection with some types of human papillomavirus. HPV infections can cause certain types of cancer, including cervical cancer in women.

HPV vaccine

HPV vaccine is routinely recommended for adolescents at 11 or 12 years of age, and the series can be started as early as age 9.

Risks of a vaccine reaction

With any medicine, including vaccines, there is a chance of reactions. These are usually mild and go away on their own, but serious reactions are also possible. Tell your health care provider about any severe allergy before the vaccine is given.
